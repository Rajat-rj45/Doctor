# MarizFirst Launch Market

## Recommended Launch Market

Primary launch city: Gurgaon

## Why Gurgaon

- Strong private healthcare ecosystem
- High search demand for doctors, clinics, hospitals, and appointment support
- Strong local SEO opportunity
- Better early partner acquisition potential
- User can manually verify providers locally before scaling

## Launch Strategy

MarizFirst will launch in one focused local market first before expanding city-wise and speciality-wise.

## Initial Area Focus

Priority areas for launch data:

1. Gurgaon
2. Sector-based locations
3. Golf Course Road
4. Golf Course Extension Road
5. Sohna Road
6. MG Road
7. New Gurgaon
8. Dwarka Expressway Gurgaon side

## Expansion Rule

Do not add multiple cities before the Gurgaon appointment funnel works reliably.

## Acceptance Criteria

- City model supports Gurgaon as launch city.
- Area/location model supports multiple Gurgaon localities.
- Public SEO pages can later support /doctors/gurgaon and /doctors/[speciality]/gurgaon.

Reason: MarizFirst documents recommend starting with one strong local market first, then scaling city-wise and speciality-wise.
