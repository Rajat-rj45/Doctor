# MarizFirst Product Positioning

MarizFirst is a trusted healthcare appointment discovery and assisted booking platform.

## What MarizFirst Is

- Healthcare appointment discovery platform
- Assisted booking system
- Verified doctor, hospital and clinic discovery product
- Patient-first appointment request funnel
- Admin/partner-confirmed booking model

## What MarizFirst Is Not

- Not a discount or coupon platform
- Not a static doctor directory
- Not an emergency care service
- Not a medical advice or diagnosis platform
- Not an auto-confirmed appointment marketplace in Phase 1

## Core Promise

Find trusted doctors, hospitals and clinics near you, choose a preferred slot, and get appointment support through MarizFirst.

## Non-Negotiable Booking Rule

Every patient appointment request starts as Pending Confirmation. The appointment becomes Confirmed only after admin or authorized partner action.

Why: Project Idea Document clearly says MarizFirst should be positioned as a trusted healthcare appointment assistance platform, not coupon/discount website.
